# Bird detection alert

`bird-alert.mp3` is a short duck quack, approximately 0.82 seconds of audio
(0.862 seconds including MP3 frame padding), mono, 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps MP3.

- Source: [single quack from a duck](https://freesound.org/people/Mikes-MultiMedia/sounds/418509/)
- Creator: Mikes-MultiMedia
- Original recording: [ducks1.wav](https://freesound.org/people/squashy555/sounds/244329/) by squashy555
- Both source pages license the recording under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Downloaded the high-quality MP3 preview linked in the source page:
  <https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/418/418509_5632532-hq.mp3>
- Changes: raised gain by 16 dB (peak about -6 dBFS), added 5 ms fade-in and
  10 ms fade-out, and re-encoded to MP3 for compatibility with older iPads.
- Retrieved November 22, 2026.

The file is bundled locally so detections do not depend on a third-party audio
request. Attribution is retained here for provenance even though CC0 does not
require it.
